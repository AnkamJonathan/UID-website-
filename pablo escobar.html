PABLO ESCOBAR

The Story of a Notorious Colombian Drug Lord

Pablo Emilio Escobar Gaviria

Born: December 1, 1949

Died: December 2, 1993

01 — Who was he?

From Medellín to the center of the cocaine trade.

Pablo Emilio Escobar Gaviria became the leader of the Medellín Cartel. During the 1980s and early 1990s, the organization became one of the world's most powerful cocaine-trafficking networks. His wealth and influence were accompanied by violence, corruption, kidnappings and attacks that caused widespread suffering in Colombia.

This page focuses on the historical record rather than glorifying his criminal activities.

03 — Timeline

How the story unfolded.

Year Event Description
1970s Rise in organized cocaine trafficking Escobar and other traffickers helped build the Medellín Cartel into a major cocaine-trafficking organization.
1980s Power, wealth and violence The cartel expanded while violence, corruption, assassinations and attacks escalated.
1991 Surrender Escobar surrendered to Colombian authorities after negotiations involving the government's policy toward extradition.
1992 Escape He escaped from La Catedral after authorities moved to relocate him to a more conventional prison.
1993 Final operation He was located in Medellín and killed by Colombian security forces on December 2, 1993.

How Did Pablo Escobar Die?

Pablo Escobar died on December 2, 1993, in Medellín, Colombia. Colombian security forces located him and a gunfight followed when he tried to escape from the building where he was hiding.

He was shot and killed during the operation, one day after his 44th birthday.
